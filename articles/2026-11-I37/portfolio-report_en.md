# My real crypto portfolio: 22 coins, entry prices and results

*BICAPITAL's portfolio management report, in percentages only: what we hold, where we bought compared with each coin's history, what happened along the way, and how it compares with simply holding bitcoin.*

This is the portfolio that runs with real money at BICAPITAL. It is not a backtest. The report shows no amounts: results are in percent, and the account is an index where the money put in equals 1,000 points. Cut-off: 5 November 2026, 20:03 UTC.

**Interactive version, with daily and weekly candles for each coin, our buys and our average price:** [test201807.github.io/bicapital-research/portfolio](https://test201807.github.io/bicapital-research/portfolio/)

## Summary

1. **Result on cost: −5.9%.** If the same money had gone into bitcoin only, on the same dates, it would be at −4.3%. The account index is at 940 points.
2. **We bought close to the lows and far from the highs.** The median altcoin was bought 70% below its highest close, at 16 out of 100 in its own closing-price history (0 is the all-time low). 19 of the 22 coins were bought in the lower half of their history, and 15 in the lowest quarter.
3. **The portfolio led and then gave it back.** On 6 October it was up 21.3% against 6.8% for bitcoin. The October fall erased that lead.
4. **The Binance hack of 29 October did not affect the portfolio.** The market fall that came with it filled our ladder of buy orders at good prices.
5. **Today:** 100% invested, spot only, no leverage, 519 trades (515 buys, 4 sells).

## How the portfolio is run

- **40 / 60 mix.** Bitcoin is capped at 40%. The other 60% is split equally across 21 altcoins (2.86% each). Since 5 November, if bitcoin goes over 40%, the excess is sold to top up the altcoins.
- **Staged entries.** Limit orders on three levels at 1.8, 3.2 and 4.5 ATR below the price, adjusted to supports, split 40 / 35 / 25.
- **On-chain gate.** An immediate buy only happens if a composite of on-chain indicators (MVRV, AVIV-Z, Mayer, Puell, Reserve Risk) falls to the 5th percentile of the last four years.
- **Due diligence per coin.** Coins with heavy token supply ahead (RED, BERA, BABY, GPS) get half the allocation, and ORCA gets no new money.
- **Holdings earn while we hold.** The positions sit in Binance Earn Flexible, which pays a yield and can be redeemed at any time. The risk taken is custody at the exchange.

## Where we bought

The question we asked of every entry was simple: compared with the whole history of the coin, did we buy low or high? For the altcoins the answer is low. Half of them were bought in the bottom sixth of their closing-price range, and 15 of the 22 coins in the bottom quarter. Bitcoin, which rises over the long run, was bought 37% below its highest close.

There are two things to read next to that. Within the last year alone, the entries sit in the middle of the range (a weighted percentile of 56). And in the coins whose 200-day average is still falling (BERA, BABY, SYRUP, VIRTUAL, TAO and SKY), the price can keep making new lows before it turns. The coins bought highest in their own history are MORPHO, bitcoin and PUMP.

## What happened

- **20 Aug:** first tranche of buys, about 5% of the programme, with the 40 / 60 mix.
- **24 Aug:** the rules are rewritten: fixed 40 / 60, the on-chain gate and the three-level buy ladder.
- **17 Sep:** the programme is fully funded. One-off exception: about 29% bought at market and the rest placed on the ladder.
- **18-22 Sep:** 22 breakout orders fill as prices rise.
- **22 and 27 Sep:** the ladder is rebuilt on ATR levels anchored to supports, then fine-tuned.
- **7 Oct:** supply review. ORCA is cut off from new money and RED, BERA, BABY and GPS go to half allocation.
- **8 Oct:** a market fall fills 10 orders, about 10% of the programme, including bitcoin at 82,006.
- **29 Oct:** [hack of Binance's hot wallets](https://www.pbs.org/newshour/world/what-to-know-about-the-6-3b-hack-on-binance-likely-the-biggest-ever-crypto-theft). Binance suspends withdrawals and covers the losses. The portfolio is not affected, and the market fall fills the ladder at good prices (about 13% of the programme).
- **5 Nov:** withdrawals reopen. Holdings move to Earn Flexible, the last 13 orders are bought at market, and a new rule caps bitcoin at 40%. The excess bitcoin (about 7% of the programme) is sold and spread across 15 altcoins.

## An honest reading

| Group | Weight | Group result | Points of the total |

| Bitcoin | 39.8% | −2.3% | −0.9 |
| SOL | 3.1% | −12.3% | −0.4 |

- **So far, spreading into altcoins has cost us against bitcoin alone:** −5.9% against −4.3%.
- **Diversification is narrower than the weights suggest.** By weight, the portfolio equals 5.7 evenly sized positions, but the average correlation of the altcoins with bitcoin is 0.51 (daily returns, 90 days), and the whole lead disappeared when the market fell.
- **The heavy-supply coins weigh 10% and explain about a quarter of the loss.** In hindsight, cutting their new money on 7 October went in the right direction.
- **The rules changed six times in 77 days.** The last change sold bitcoin below its average price to top up the altcoins. We publish it as a record of learning, not as a finished system.
